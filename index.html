How it works

Three clear steps from silent video upload to synchronized cinematic Foley.

1

Upload your footage

Drop any silent MP4, MOV, or WebM video up to sixty seconds into the studio interface. Set your desired acoustic intensity and room style.

2

Vision analysis and optical snapping

Gemini Vision logs physical contact events while optical flow algorithms calculate precise frame velocities to snap each event to the exact collision moment.

3

Acoustic synthesis and mastering

Generative engines synthesize high fidelity audio, apply environmental convolution reverb, and master the output with peak limiting at 0.95.
